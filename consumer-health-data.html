Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

For people in the United States. Some US state laws (among them Washington's My Health My Data Act, and similar laws in Nevada and Connecticut) give you rights over "consumer health data". This page explains what of that kind Taste10 holds. Our full Privacy Policy covers everything else.

Who we are

Taste10 is provided by Perelli GmbH, Dorfstrasse 5, 6318 Walchwil, Switzerland. Contact: privacy (at) taste10.com.

What consumer health data we collect

Taste10 predicts taste. It does not ask about, store or check allergies, intolerances or other health conditions.

What Taste10 does store is the diets you choose (vegetarian, vegan, pescatarian, low carb, no alcohol, no pork, no beef, no red meat, low sugar), a history of changes to them, and what the app works out about a scanned product in relation to them (for example that it may not match your diet). These are lifestyle choices and we do not use them to draw conclusions about your health. Because some state laws define consumer health data broadly, we treat them with the protections on this page anyway.

Where it comes from

Only from you, in the app. We do not buy health data or receive it from other companies.

Why we collect and use it

Only to provide what you ask Taste10 for: personal predictions, and pointing out on each scan and each suggestion what may not match your diet. We do not use it for advertising, and we do not sell it.

Your choice

Choosing a diet is optional, and the app works without one. You can change or remove your diets at any time in the app (Profile), or write to us.

Who we share it with

We do not sell consumer health data. It is processed on our behalf, and only to provide Taste10, by:

  • Supabase, which stores it in our database (in the European Union);
  • Our AI provider, today OpenAI, which receives it with each scan to produce your result (processed in the United States). It does not use the data to train its models. If we change the AI provider, we name the new one here and in our Privacy Policy no later than the day it starts receiving data.

We would disclose it to others only if the law required us to.

Your rights

You can:

  • ask whether we hold your consumer health data, and get a copy;
  • ask for the list of companies we have shared it with;
  • change or remove your diets at any time;
  • have it deleted. Deleting your account in the app (Profile, then Delete account) deletes it, or write to us.

Write to privacy (at) taste10.com from the email address on your account. We answer within 45 days. If we turn a request down, you can appeal by replying to our answer with the word "appeal"; if the appeal is also refused, you can contact your state's Attorney General (in Washington, through atg.wa.gov).
