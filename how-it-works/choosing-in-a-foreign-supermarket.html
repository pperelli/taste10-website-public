How Taste10 Works

Choosing in a foreign supermarket

By the Taste10 team · Updated November 2026

In a supermarket abroad, or in the international aisle at home, many products are hard to judge from the packet. Photograph a product, or a whole shelf, and Taste10 tells you in your language what it is, what it tastes like and how well it fits your personal taste.

How it works

  1. Photograph the product: the front, or the front and the back with the ingredients, up to three photos at once.
  2. Or photograph a whole shelf. Taste10 picks out the products it can see and shows which ones suit you best.
  3. See what it is, explained in plain words in your language, and a personal score from 0 to 10 with the reason why.

What it helps with

  • Labels you cannot read, in Japanese, Thai, Korean, Greek and many other languages.
  • Products you have never seen: a snack, a sauce, a drink or a fruit you do not know.
  • Choosing between similar products: five kinds of crisps or ten kinds of noodles.

Tips

  • Make the front of the pack clearly visible, with the name and the picture.
  • Add a photo of the back when you want Taste10 to look at the ingredients too.
  • Rate it once you have tried it, so your next scan fits you even better.

Questions and answers

Can Taste10 read labels in other languages?

Yes. It reads labels in many languages and scripts and tells you in your app language what the product is and how well it fits you.

Can I scan a whole shelf?

Yes. Photograph a shelf with several products and Taste10 shows which of the products it can see suit you best.

Does Taste10 work in my supermarket at home?

Yes. It works with any product you can photograph, at home or abroad.
