How Taste10 Works

Taste10 gets to know your taste, then tells you how much you are likely to enjoy a food, a dish or a whole menu before you try it. These pages show how.

  • Your taste portrait: what it shows and how it growsYour Taste10 taste portrait is the picture Taste10 has of your palate: your taste archetype, your taste patterns and foods you might love. It grows as you rate.
  • Choosing in a foreign supermarketPhotograph a product or a whole shelf in a foreign supermarket and see what it is, what it tastes like and how well it fits your own taste.
  • Reading a menu in a language you don't speakPhotograph a menu in any language, even in a script you cannot read, and see what each dish is and which ones fit your own taste best.
  • How Taste10 learns your tasteHow Taste10 gets to know your personal taste: a short setup, quick questions about foods, and your ratings of what you actually tried.
  • Why the same food can be a 9 for you and a 3 for someone elseTaste is personal: genes, experience and habit shape what you enjoy. Why a personal taste score predicts more for you than an average star rating.

Find out what fits your taste

Taste10 learns your personal taste and predicts how much you will enjoy a food, drink, dish or menu before you try it, as a score from 0 to 10 with the reason why.
