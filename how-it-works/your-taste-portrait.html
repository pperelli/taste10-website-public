How Taste10 Works

Your taste portrait: what it shows and how it grows

By the Taste10 team · Updated November 2026

Your taste portrait is the picture Taste10 has of your palate: your taste archetype, the flavours and textures you lean towards, what stands out about you, and foods you might love. It appears after the setup and grows every time you answer a question or rate something you tried.

What your taste portrait shows

  • Your taste archetype, a name such as The Explorer or The Bitter Enthusiast, with a line about what it means.
  • Your taste fingerprint: how much you enjoy sweet, sour, bitter, salty, savoury and spicy, and which textures you like.
  • What stands out: short observations about your palate, for example that you like sour and spicy together, or that you go looking for strong cheeses.
  • You might love: foods you have not tried yet that are likely to suit you.
  • Your Taste Insights: how much you have taught Taste10 so far.

How it grows

At first your portrait is based on how you described your own taste. As you answer quick questions and rate what you actually ate or drank, it moves from what you said to what you showed. Many people discover something new about their own taste along the way.

Where to find it

You see your portrait for the first time at the end of the setup. After that, tap your Taste Insights count at the top of the app, or open it in the Kitchen tab, any time. You can also share it with friends as a picture.

Why it matters

The portrait is not just a fun summary. It is the same picture of your taste that Taste10 uses for every score, so the clearer it gets, the better your scores fit you.

Find out what fits your taste

Taste10 learns your personal taste, shows you what it has learned in your taste portrait, and predicts how much you will enjoy any food, drink, dish or menu, with the reason why.
