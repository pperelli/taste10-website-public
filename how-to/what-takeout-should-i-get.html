What should I get for dinner? How to choose takeout you'll enjoy

By the Taste10 team · Updated November 2026

Decide your mood first (comfort, fresh, spicy or something new), pick one cuisine that matches it, then choose a dish that travels well and suits your own taste. Most takeout regret comes from tired, last-minute scrolling, the same order out of habit, or food that went soggy on the way.

Takeout ideas by mood

Start with how you feel, not with the app's long list of restaurants. One word for your mood narrows hundreds of options to two or three cuisines.

Why choosing takeout is so hard in the evening

A delivery app shows more choice than any restaurant menu: hundreds of places, each with dozens of dishes. A review of 99 studies on choice overload by Alexander Chernev and colleagues found that too many options make choosing harder mainly under certain conditions: when you are unsure what you prefer, and when you want to decide with as little effort as possible. Both describe a tired evening on the sofa almost exactly. That is why it helps to settle one thing at a time (mood, then cuisine, then dish) instead of scrolling everything at once.

5 steps to choose takeout in five minutes

  1. Name your mood in one word. Comfort, fresh, spicy, something new: the table above turns it into cuisines.
  2. Pick the cuisine before the restaurant. Then compare three restaurants at most, not thirty.
  3. Read past the star rating. Four and a half stars means many people liked the food, not that you will. Reviews that describe the taste ("very sweet", "really spicy", "lots of garlic") tell you more than the number.
  4. Choose something that travels well. A great dish that arrives soggy is a disappointing dinner (see the table below).
  5. Try one new dish beside your usual one. Ordering the same thing every time is easy, but one new dish a week is how you find your next favourite without risking the whole meal.

What travels well, and what does not

Delivery adds time and a closed box. Some dishes do not mind; others lose what made them good. A rough guide:

DishHow it survives delivery
Curries and stewsTravel very well; many taste just as good a little later
Fried rice and stir-fried noodlesTravel well; the box keeps them warm
PizzaTravels fairly well; the base softens a little on longer trips
Burritos and wrapsTravel well; the wrapping holds everything together
Fries and anything crisp-friedSuffer most: steam in a closed box softens the crust
Noodle soups such as ramen or phoFine only when the broth comes separately; otherwise the noodles soak it up
Dressed saladsWilt on the way unless the dressing comes on the side

If fries do go soft, a few minutes in a hot oven or a dry pan brings back more crunch than the microwave.

Questions and answers

What should I get for dinner if I cannot decide?

Name your mood in one word, such as comfort, fresh or spicy, pick one cuisine that matches it, and choose between three dishes at most. Fewer options make the decision quick.

What takeout travels best?

Curries, stews, fried rice and stir-fried noodles travel best. Fries and crisp-fried food suffer most, because steam in the closed box softens the crust.

Is the highest-rated dish the best choice?

It is the dish most people liked, not necessarily the one you will like most. Reviews that describe the taste help you judge whether it suits you.
