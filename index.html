How much will you like it?

Taste10 learns what you enjoy, then predicts how well something fits your taste — before you try it. Photograph a product, a dish or a whole menu.

A score for you, not a review

Taste10 gives a match from 0 to 10. It is a prediction about you, never a judgement of quality: an excellent product can honestly score 3 if it clashes with your taste, and the everyday thing you love can score 9.

Your allergies come first

Diets and allergies are checked separately from taste, and they always win. If something does not fit, you see that above the score, and Taste10 never suggests anything your diet or allergies rule out.

It reads what it can see and tells you how sure it is. That is a helpful first check, not a guarantee — if you have an allergy, always check the real label or ask the staff.

It gets to know you

You start with a short setup. After that, quick questions about individual foods, and your ratings of things you actually ate, sharpen the predictions. Your taste portrait — the picture Taste10 has of your palate — changes as it learns.
