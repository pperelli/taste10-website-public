2. What Taste10 does, and what it does not

Taste10 estimates how well a food, a drink or a dish is likely to suit your personal taste, and tells you what it believes a product contains. Those are estimates produced by software, largely from photographs. They can be incomplete or simply wrong: a label may be unreadable, a recipe may have changed, a kitchen may not do what the menu says, and a photograph cannot show substitutions, traces or cross-contact.

Taste10 is not medical advice, not an allergen detector and not a safety device. It does not diagnose, treat or prevent anything. If you have an allergy, an intolerance or any medical condition, follow your doctor's advice, read the actual packaging, and ask the people serving you. The app says the same wherever an allergen is shown.
